# Apprentice Induction Manual - SENA

Welcome to the SENA virtual induction process. This document guides you step-by-step to successfully complete your induction using our interactive platform.

## 1. Access to the Platform
To begin, access the link shared by your instructor. Once the page loads, you will see the welcome screen.
You can use the **Language Switcher (ES / EN)** button in the top right corner at any time to toggle between Spanish and English for the entire platform.

## 2. Role Selection
On the welcome screen, select the **Apprentice** button (or **Instructor** if you are the lead instructor using the access PIN `Sena 1234`).

## 3. Registration and Profiling
Once the apprentice role is selected, the system will prompt you to complete your profiling form:
* **Full Name:** Enter your name as it appears on your identity document.
* **ID Document:** Enter your identification number.
* **Cohort Number (Ficha):** Enter your assigned SENA cohort number.
* **WhatsApp Number:** Enter your contact phone number to receive compliance reminders, module guides, and alerts.

Click on the **Start Induction** button to access the main induction path.

## 4. Platform Navigation
At the top navigation bar you will find the main sections:

* **Induction Path:** Access the 6 institutional training modules (SENA Identity, Pedagogical Model, Digital Platforms, Wellbeing & Opportunities, FPI Process, and Apprentice Regulations).
* **Regulation Tribunal:** Solve 9 ethical dilemmas and cases based on the 2024 Apprentice Regulations and 2026 Complementary Agreement to practice pedagogical judgment and preventive measures.
* **Level Challenges:** Test your speed and knowledge in timed question challenges to earn XP points and level up.
* **SENA Glossary:** Look up key concepts, regulatory terminology, digital platforms (SofiaPlus, Zajuna), and academic terms.
* **ID & Certificate:** View your real-time level progress, digital ID card, and download your official Induction Certificate once requirements are satisfied.
* **SENA Tutor:** Consult the virtual pedagogical advisor powered by Gemini AI for instant guidance on regulations and learning processes.
* **YouTube Channel:** Click on the YouTube icon to visit Instructor Blas Thorrens's channel (`@blasthorrensmendoza3413`) for official video tutorials.

## 5. Progress and Notifications
* **Progress & Gamification:** Every completed module and tribunal case earns XP points and unlocks badges. You can monitor your global progress bar and level progress in the header.
* **WhatsApp Alerts:** You will receive automated WhatsApp notifications when you complete modules or when new learning guides are available.
* **Google Drive Sync:** If your instructor has configured Google Drive synchronization, your progress and grades are updated automatically in the master class sheet.

## 6. Key Recommendations
* **Responsibility:** Induction is mandatory and the use of SENA platforms is personal and non-transferable.
* **AI Support:** If you have questions about regulations or procedures, consult the **SENA Tutor** (Bot) for immediate support in Spanish or English.
* **Bilingual Support:** The entire repository and interface seamlessly supports both Spanish and English.

Best wishes in your comprehensive vocational training process at SENA!
